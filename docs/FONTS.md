# Self-hosted fonts

- Lora, regular and bold. Supplied licensed local collection, converted to WOFF2 with fontTools with unchanged outlines. SIL OFL 1.1 in `fonts/Lora-OFL.txt`.
- Work Sans, regular and bold. Supplied licensed local collection, converted to WOFF2 with fontTools with unchanged outlines. SIL OFL 1.1 in `fonts/WorkSans-OFL.txt`.
- IBM Plex Mono, regular and bold. Supplied licensed local collection, converted to WOFF2 with fontTools with unchanged outlines. SIL OFL 1.1 in `fonts/IBMPlexMono-OFL.txt`.

No network downloads. Two static weights or a variable weight axis are included.
